Echoes Of The Lost Planet

3D Platformer

A 3D endless runner where players explore a small planet, collect crystals to increase their score, and avoid incoming obstacles. The objective is to survive for as long as possible while achieving the highest score

Unity

C#

3D

My Contribution

  • Developed the high score system.
  • Designed gameplay levels and progression.
  • Created 3D environmental models.
  • I came up with the game's concept.
  • Conducted gameplay testing and debugging.

Key Features

  • Endless runner gameplay on a spherical planet
  • Crystal collection and high score system
  • Progressive gameplay
  • Offline gameplay

Challenges

The biggest challenges were developing the game within an 8-hour time limit, maintaining effective team communication, and avoiding merge conflicts while collaborating through GitHub.

Solutions

We divided tasks based on each team member's responsibilities, communicated regularly to track progress, and used GitHub carefully by working on separate branches and frequently syncing changes to minimize merge conflicts. This allowed us to complete the project within the limited time.

What I Learned

This project improved my ability to work under tight deadlines while collaborating in a team. I learned the importance of task delegation, clear communication, version control using GitHub, and making efficient development decisions to deliver a complete game within a short timeframe.

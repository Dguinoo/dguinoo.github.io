Road Ready

3D Driving Simulator

A 3D driving game that is set in Naga City, Camarines Sur. This game teaches newbie and unlicensed drivers about traffic sign and road rules.

Unity

C#

3D

My Contribution

  • Designed driving routes based on Naga City roads to teach and test players' understanding of Philippine LTO road signs.
  • Built a 5km² open-world terrain with 50+ 3D assets (buildings, trees, road props) to design progressive driving routes that test players' understanding of Philippine traffic laws.
  • Modeled 3D vehicles (jeepney, Etrike, Car, Ebike, bike) and environmental assets to maximize immersion and replayability.
  • Optimized game assets to reduce memory usage and improve performance.
  • Identified gameplay bugs.
  • Tested vehicle physics.
  • Verified collision detection and checkpoint logic.

Key Features

  • 3D driving simulation
  • Philippine traffic sign recognition
  • Educational learning mode
  • Interactive driving course
  • Offline gameplay
  • Progression System

Challenges

I encountered a challenge that was optimizing the game for lower-end computers without significantly reducing visual quality and how to improve 3d models.

Solutions

To improve performance, I optimized the 3D models by converting them to low-poly versions, reduced unnecessary lighting calculations, implemented mesh compression on the 3D models, simplified shaders, and reused materials to reduce draw calls.

What I Learned

This project strengthened my skills in 3D game development, debugging, optimization, and collaboration within a development team. Despite it being my first project as a 3D artist, I also gained experience integrating assets and designing systems that balance performance with player experience, gaining valuable hands-on experience in developing and optimizing a 3D game.

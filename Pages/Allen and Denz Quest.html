Allen & Denz Quest

2D Side-Scrolling Platformer

Allen and Denz Quest is a 2D puzzle platformer game with a full of traps. Work together with your partner to overcome challenges and complete the adventure.

Unity

C#

2D

My Contribution

  • Designed cooperative puzzle levels requiring teamwork and coordination.
  • Balanced gameplay for a two-player cooperative experience.
  • Built and programmed traps and interactive level mechanics.
  • Created obstacles and environmental gameplay challenges.

Key Features

  • Two-player cooperative gameplay
  • Puzzle and obstacle-based levels
  • Progressive level system
  • Offline multiplayer

Challenges

One of the biggest challenges was designing puzzle levels that were both engaging and efficient to create. Each level needed to encourage cooperation between two players while maintaining a balanced difficulty and a smooth progression.

Solutions

I used Unity's Tilemap system to speed up level creation and make iteration easier. By rapidly prototyping layouts and testing them with my teammate, I refined puzzles, obstacle placement, and trap mechanics to create a more enjoyable cooperative experience.

What I Learned

This project strengthened my understanding of cooperative game design and level design principles. I learned how to create puzzles that require teamwork, use Unity's Tilemap system to develop levels more efficiently, and improve gameplay through continuous playtesting and iteration.

ElemenTical

Educational

Elementical is an educational mobile game where players scan physical QR-coded cards to answer timed multiple-choice questions, supporting up to 8 players on one device.

Unity

C#

2D

My Contribution

  • Helped brainstorm the game's design and overall direction.
  • Designed the user interface and buttons.
  • Assisted in designing the physical QR-coded cards.
  • Helped refine the game's visual style and layout.

Key Features

  • Educational gameplay
  • Interactive QR card system
  • Timed multiple-choice questions
  • Up to 8-player local gameplay
  • Physical cards with QR codes
  • Offline gameplay

Challenges

One of the biggest challenges was meeting the clinic's design expectations. The design went through multiple revisions because the client provided frequent feedback and requested changes to the UI and physical card designs.

Solutions

I worked closely with the team to apply the client's feedback and make revisions efficiently. We presented updated designs regularly, discussed the changes with the client, and refined the UI and card designs until they met the required expectations.

What I Learned

This project taught me the importance of adapting to client feedback and being flexible during the design process. I learned how to communicate design ideas, handle multiple revisions, and create designs that balance both user experience and client requirements.

Fractured Spectrum

3D Top-down Survival Shooter

As a lone glowing icosahedron in a geometric void, you face endless waves of color-coded enemies—fragments of a broken spectrum. Survive, or be shattered.

Unity

C#

3D

My Contribution

  • Programmed player movement, jumping, and combat mechanics.
  • Implemented enemy AI with player detection, tracking, and unique behaviors using finite state machines.
  • Created unique behaviors for each enemy type.
  • Developed the wave-based gameplay system with timed enemy spawning.
  • Built the game's progression system, including level flow, enemy waves, and scene transitions.
  • Balanced enemy encounters to create a progressive gameplay experience.
  • Implemented object pooling for bullets to improve performance.
  • Created particle effects for explosions and combat feedback.
  • Developed URP lighting and emissive visual effects.
  • Defined the game's visual direction to maintain a consistent art style.

Key Features

  • Wave-based survival system
  • Progressive gameplay and level progression
  • Multiple enemy types with unique AI behaviors/li>
  • Offline gameplay
  • Optimized performance using object pooling

Challenges

The biggest challenge was transitioning my game development knowledge from 2D to 3D. I had to adapt familiar gameplay mechanics, enemy AI, and level design concepts to a three-dimensional environment while learning new workflows.

Solutions

I applied the programming principles and gameplay systems I had learned from 2D development, then adapted them to 3D by experimenting, prototyping, and iterating. Through continuous testing and problem-solving, I successfully implemented the mechanics in a 3D environment.

What I Learned

This was my first solo 3D game inspired by the gameplay of Vampire Survivors. The project strengthened my understanding of 3D game development, reinforced that many core programming concepts apply across both 2D and 3D games, and improved my confidence in building complete games independently.

Cat's Revenge

2D Platformer Shooter

In this 2D pixel platformer, play as a fearless cat who travels to the past to defeat a mutated dog and prevent the world’s destruction.

Unity

C#

2D

My Contribution

  • Programmed player movement, jumping, and combat mechanics.
  • Developed the Health (HP) and Bullet resource systems, including collectible items for health and ammunition recovery.
  • Implemented enemy AI with player detection, tracking, and combat behaviors using finite state machines.
  • Designed and programmed a multi-phase boss with unique attack patterns and health-based behavior changes.
  • Designed enemy behaviors and balanced combat encounters to create a progressive gameplay experience.
  • Built the game's progression system, including level flow, enemy waves, and scene transitions.
  • Balanced gameplay difficulty by adjusting enemy health, damage, and player progression.
  • Created all pixel art assets in Piskel, including the player, enemies, boss, environment tiles, and UI sprites.
  • Defined the game's visual direction to maintain a consistent pixel art style throughout the project.

Challenges

I encountered a challenge that was optimizing the game for lower-end computers without significantly reducing visual quality and how to improve 3d models.

Solutions

I addressed this challenge by designing a clear state machine with well-defined transitions between each enemy state. Player detection was handled through distance checks and trigger colliders, allowing enemies to switch from patrolling to chasing or attacking at the appropriate time. I synchronized the AI states with the Animator Controller by updating animation parameters whenever the enemy changed behavior. Extensive testing and debugging helped eliminate animation conflicts, resulting in smooth AI behavior and responsive enemy reactions.

What I Learned

This challenge strengthened my understanding of finite state machines and AI behavior design in Unity. I learned how to organize AI logic into manageable states, synchronize gameplay logic with animations, and debug complex state transitions effectively. It also taught me the importance of writing modular, maintainable code and thoroughly testing edge cases to ensure reliable enemy behavior throughout the game.

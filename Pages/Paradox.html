Paradox

2D Platformer

You are a simple sphere—a spark of consciousness trapped in a tiered reality—driven to ascend from the heat of the depths toward the calm of the sky, never knowing whether reaching the top is escape, illusion, or something far more unsettling.

Challenges

The biggest challenge was starting with Unity without any prior experience. Learning the engine, understanding its components, and implementing core gameplay mechanics from scratch required significant time and experimentation.

Solutions

I learned Unity through official tutorials, online resources, and continuous practice. By building small prototypes and experimenting with different features, I gradually gained the skills needed to develop the game's movement, level design, and gameplay systems.

What I Learned

This was the first game I created in Unity and my first project centered around a bouncing ball mechanic. It gave me a strong foundation in Unity, game programming, physics-based gameplay, and the overall game development process.

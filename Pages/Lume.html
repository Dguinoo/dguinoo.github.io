Challenges

One of the biggest challenges was importing 3D models from Maya into Unity. Although the low poly models appeared smooth in Maya, their shading and normals looked incorrect after being imported into Unity.

Solutions

I learned how to troubleshoot model import settings by recalculating normals, adjusting smoothing groups, and configuring the appropriate FBX export and Unity import settings. Through testing and iteration, I achieved the intended visual quality in Unity.

What I Learned

This was my first 3D game project, and it introduced me to the complete 3D asset pipeline from modeling in Maya to importing, optimizing, and integrating assets into Unity. It helped me build a strong foundation in 3D game development and asset optimization.
